## Logo Usage Guidelines

### Sizes
- **Large:** 72-96px (Login, splash screens)
- **Medium:** 40-56px (Headers, navigation)
- **Small:** 24-32px (Favicons, mobile)

### Spacing
- Minimum clear space: 8px around logo
- Don't place on busy backgrounds
- Ensure good contrast

### Colors
- Primary: Use gradient as shown
- Monochrome: Use single color when needed
- Dark mode: Lighten colors slightly
